Frequently Asked Questions

Can a complete beginner join RecGetup Music? expand_more
Absolutely! RecGetup Music offers specialized foundation courses for beginners that focus on basic pitch, rhythm, and breath control.
What is the format of the classes at RecGetup? expand_more
Classes are held over high-definition interactive platforms. They include live demonstrations, interactive practice sessions, and personalized corrections from our mentors.
Does RecGetup Music provide certification? expand_more
Yes, upon successful completion of each level and assessment, students receive a digital certificate of achievement from RecGetup Music.
